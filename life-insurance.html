❓ FAQ

▶
▶ What is the DIME method?
DIME stands for Debt, Income, Mortgage, and Education. It's a simple way to calculate life insurance needs: add all debts, plus annual income × years needed, plus mortgage, plus education costs, then subtract existing savings.
▶ How many years should I cover?
A common rule is to cover until your youngest child reaches age 22-25 (college completion). Or use 20-30 years for general income replacement.
▶ What about inflation?
This calculator uses today's dollars. For a more accurate estimate, consider increasing your coverage amount by 2-3% per year to account for inflation over time.
▶ Is term or whole life better?
Term life is cheaper and provides pure protection for a set period — ideal for most families. Whole life costs more but builds cash value. Choose based on your budget and goals.
▶ Should I include my spouse's income?
Yes — if your spouse also contributes to household income, factor that in. This calculator uses your household expenses, which assumes both incomes are needed to cover them.
