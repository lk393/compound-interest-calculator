❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is DTI (Debt-to-Income Ratio)?
DTI is the percentage of your monthly gross income that goes toward debt payments. Lenders typically prefer a DTI below 36% for mortgage approval.
▶What if my calculated loan amount is too low?
You can increase your down payment, extend the loan term (which lowers monthly payments), reduce your existing debts, or look for a lower interest rate.
