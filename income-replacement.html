❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is a realistic income replacement ratio?
Most financial advisors recommend 70-80% of your pre-retirement income. Higher earners may need a lower percentage, while lower earners may need a higher percentage.
▶Why not 100% of my pre-retirement income?
In retirement, you no longer pay payroll taxes, save for retirement, or commute to work. These expenses typically reduce your required income by 20-30%.
