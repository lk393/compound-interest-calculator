❓ FAQ

▶How much do I need to invest monthly to reach $1 million in 20 years at 8%?
You need to invest about $1,700 per month. At 8% annual return, your $408,000 in contributions would grow to $1,000,000 — earning $592,000 in returns.
▶How much will $500/month grow to in 30 years at 7%?
Saving $500/month at 7% for 30 years grows to approximately $566,764. Your $180,000 in contributions earns $386,764 in returns.
▶What return rate should I use for long-term investing?
Historical stock market returns average 7-10% per year. Use 7% for conservative planning, 10% for optimistic. For a 20-year goal, 8% is a reasonable middle-ground.
