📖 How to read your results

▶

Required Monthly Contribution

The amount you need to invest each month to reach your goal.

Total Contributions

The total amount you will contribute out of pocket over the investment period.

Total Investment Returns

The total earnings generated by your investments — the difference between your goal and total contributions.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is a realistic expected return?
For long-term stock investments, 7-10% is common. Use a lower rate (5-6%) for more conservative planning.
▶Does this include taxes or fees?
No — this calculation does not account for taxes, management fees, or transaction costs. Adjust your expected return accordingly.
