📖 How to read your results

▶

Base Currency

The currency you choose for reporting. All income and expenses are converted to this currency.

Exchange Rate

The rate you enter to convert each foreign currency to the base currency.

Net Balance

Total income minus total expenses in the base currency. Positive means you're in surplus.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶How do I budget with income in USD and expenses in EUR?
Convert all amounts to your base currency using exchange rates. For example, $5,000 USD income at 1.05 rate = €5,250. Expenses: €3,000 = $2,857. Net = $2,143 USD.
▶What exchange rate should I use for budgeting?
Use the mid-market rate for planning, but add a buffer (1-2%) to account for currency fluctuations. For a $5,000 budget, a 2% fluctuation means $100 difference.
▶How can I reduce currency risk in my budget?
Hold a buffer in your base currency, use forward contracts for large amounts, or diversify currency exposure. For a monthly budget of $10,000, a 5% rate move can change your budget by $500.
