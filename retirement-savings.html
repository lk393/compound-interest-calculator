📖 How to read your results

▶

Projected Savings

The total amount you'll have by retirement age, including all your contributions and the compounded investment returns.

Total Contributions

The total amount you contributed out of pocket — your current savings plus all monthly contributions over the years.

Total Investment Returns

The total earnings generated by your investments. This is the difference between your projected savings and your total contributions.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶ How realistic is the projected return?
A 7% annual return is a common historical average for stock-heavy portfolios. Use a lower rate (5-6%) for more conservative estimates.
▶ Does this include inflation?
No — this calculation shows nominal dollars. To adjust for inflation, use a lower expected return (e.g., 5% instead of 7%) to see your purchasing power.
▶ What if I increase my contributions later?
This calculator assumes consistent monthly contributions. You can try different scenarios by adjusting the monthly contribution amount and recalculating.
▶ How do I know if I'm saving enough?
Compare your projected savings to your target retirement number. A common rule of thumb is to aim for 25x your annual retirement expenses (the 4% rule).
