❓ FAQ

▶
▶What will $10,000 grow to in 20 years at 8% annual return?
A $10,000 lump sum investment at 8% annual return will grow to approximately $46,610 in 20 years — more than 4.6x your original investment.
▶Is it better to invest a lump sum or dollar-cost average?
Historically, lump sum investing beats dollar-cost averaging about 70% of the time in rising markets. A $10,000 lump sum at 7% for 10 years grows to $19,672, while monthly investing $833 at 7% grows to $17,041.
▶How much should I invest to reach $1 million in 25 years?
At 7% return, you'd need to invest about $184,000 as a lump sum today. At 10% return, you'd need about $92,000.
