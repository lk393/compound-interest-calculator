📖 How to read your results

▶

Final Value

The total value of your investment at the end of the period, including all compounded returns.

Total Return

The total profit earned — Final Value minus your initial investment.

CAGR

The steady annual growth rate that would produce the same final value over the same period.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is CAGR?
CAGR (Compound Annual Growth Rate) is the annualized return that smooths out volatility. It's the rate your investment would need to grow each year to reach the final value.
▶Does this account for inflation?
No — this shows nominal returns. To see real (inflation-adjusted) returns, subtract the inflation rate from your expected return.
