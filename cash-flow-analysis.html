📖 How to read your results

▶

Net Cash Flow

Your monthly surplus (income minus expenses). Positive means you're saving money; negative means you're spending more than you earn.

Savings Rate

The percentage of your income that you save each month. A healthy rate is typically 20% or higher.

Annual Cash Flow

Your net cash flow projected over 12 months. This helps you plan for annual savings goals.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is a good savings rate?
A savings rate of 20% or more is generally considered good. Higher rates help you reach financial goals faster.
▶How can I improve my cash flow?
Increase your income (side jobs, raises) or reduce expenses (cut discretionary spending, refinance debt).
