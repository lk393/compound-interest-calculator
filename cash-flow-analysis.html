❓ FAQ

▶
▶How do I calculate my monthly cash flow?
Monthly cash flow = total income - total expenses. If you have $5,000 in income and $4,000 in expenses, your cash flow is +$1,000 (positive). If expenses exceed income, you have negative cash flow.
▶What is a good cash flow percentage?
A positive cash flow of 15-20% of your income is considered good. With a $5,000 income, that means $750-$1,000 in surplus each month. Anything above 20% is excellent.
▶What causes negative cash flow and how can I fix it?
Negative cash flow occurs when expenses exceed income. Fix it by: reducing discretionary spending (eating out, subscriptions), refinancing high-interest debt, or increasing income through side work. Even a $100 reduction in expenses can turn a negative cash flow positive.
