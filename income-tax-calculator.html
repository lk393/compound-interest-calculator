❓ FAQ

▶
▶How much federal tax will I pay on $60,000 income with standard deduction?
For a single filer in 2026, taxable income = $60,000 - $14,600 = $45,400. Tax = $1,160 + ($45,400 - $11,600) × 12% = $5,216. Effective rate is 8.7%.
▶What is the 2026 standard deduction amount?
The 2026 standard deduction is $14,600 for single filers, $29,200 for married filing jointly, and $21,900 for heads of household.
▶How do I calculate my effective tax rate?
Effective tax rate = total tax ÷ total income × 100. If you earn $60,000 and pay $5,216 in tax, your effective rate is 8.7% — much lower than your marginal rate.
