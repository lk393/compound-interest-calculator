❓ FAQ

▶
▶How much will my 401(k) grow if I contribute $500/month with 5% employer match for 30 years at 7%?
Your $500/month contribution + $250/month employer match = $750/month total. At 7% return, your 401(k) would grow to approximately $850,146 in 30 years.
▶What is the 401(k) contribution limit for 2026?
The 2026 401(k) contribution limit is $23,000 ($30,500 if age 50+). If you max out at $23,000/year for 30 years at 7%, you'd have about $2.17 million.
▶What is a good employer match percentage?
A common match is 50-100% of contributions up to 3-6% of salary. A 100% match on the first 5% of salary is excellent. Every dollar of match is free money.
▶Should I contribute to 401(k) or Roth IRA first?
Contribute enough to get the full employer match in your 401(k) first — that's a 50-100% return. Then max out a Roth IRA ($7,000/year) for tax-free growth.
