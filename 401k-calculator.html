❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is employer match?
Employer match is when your employer contributes money to your retirement account based on your contributions. For example, a 50% match means your employer adds 50 cents for every $1 you contribute (up to a limit).
▶Should I always contribute enough to get the full match?
Yes — employer match is essentially free money. Always contribute at least enough to get the full match before investing elsewhere.
