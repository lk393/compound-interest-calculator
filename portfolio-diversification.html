📖 How to read your results

▶

HHI Score

Ranges from 0 (perfectly diversified) to 10,000 (single asset). Lower scores = better diversification.

Equivalent Assets

The effective number of equally‑weighted assets that would give the same HHI.

Level

HHI < 1,500 is "Well Diversified"; 1,500‑2,500 is "Moderately Concentrated"; > 2,500 is "Concentrated".

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is a good HHI score?
Below 1,500 is considered well‑diversified. Above 2,500 suggests concentration risk.
▶What is concentration risk?
The risk that a single asset or a small number of assets dominate your portfolio. Poor diversification increases this risk.
