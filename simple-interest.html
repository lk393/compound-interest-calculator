❓ FAQ

▶
▶How much interest will I earn on $10,000 at 5% simple interest for 3 years?
Using the formula Interest = Principal × Rate × Time, you would earn $1,500 in interest ($10,000 × 0.05 × 3 = $1,500). Your total balance would be $11,500.
▶What is the difference between simple and compound interest?
Simple interest is calculated only on the original principal. Compound interest is calculated on the principal plus accumulated interest. On $10,000 at 5% for 10 years, simple interest earns $5,000, while compound interest earns about $6,288 — a $1,288 difference.
▶When is simple interest used in real life?
Simple interest is commonly used for short-term loans, car loans, bonds, and certificates of deposit (CDs). Most mortgages and credit cards use compound interest.
