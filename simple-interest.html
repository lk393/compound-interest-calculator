❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is the difference between simple and compound interest?
Simple interest is calculated only on the original principal. Compound interest is calculated on the principal plus any accumulated interest from previous periods.
▶When is simple interest used?
Simple interest is commonly used for short-term loans, bonds, certificates of deposit (CDs), and some savings accounts where interest is not reinvested.
