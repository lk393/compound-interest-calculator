📖 How to read your results

▶

Monthly Benefit

The amount you would receive each month if disabled, typically 50‑70% of your pre‑disability income.

Waiting Period

The time you must be disabled before benefits start. Longer waiting periods lower premiums.

Benefit Period

How long benefits are paid. Longer periods mean more protection but higher premiums.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What does disability insurance cover?
It replaces a portion of your income if you become unable to work due to illness or injury.
▶When should I buy disability insurance?
As early as possible, ideally when you start your career. Premiums are lower when you're younger and healthier.
