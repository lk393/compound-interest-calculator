❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is the return on a stock bought at $50 and sold at $75 in 3 years?
Total return = 50% ($25 profit). CAGR = (75/50)^(1/3) - 1 = 14.5% per year. This beats the S&P 500 average of 10%.
▶What is a good stock return rate?
The S&P 500 has returned about 10% per year on average. A good stock picker aims for 15-20% per year. Warren Buffett's Berkshire Hathaway averages about 20% over 50+ years.
▶How do dividends affect total stock return?
Total return = price appreciation + dividends. A stock that grows 8% and pays 3% dividends has an 11% total return. On $10,000, that's $1,100 vs $800 in growth alone.
