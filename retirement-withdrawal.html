❓ FAQ

▶
▶How long will $500,000 last at $30,000/year with 6% return?
Your money will last about 28.5 years. At 6% return, your $500,000 grows enough to sustain $30,000 withdrawals for nearly 3 decades.
▶How much can I withdraw from $1 million at 4% inflation-adjusted?
Using the 4% rule, you can withdraw $40,000 in year one, then increase with inflation (about 3% yearly). Your money should last at least 30 years.
▶What happens if I withdraw too much from my retirement savings?
Withdrawing too much depletes your savings faster. Withdrawing $50,000/year from a $500,000 portfolio at 6% return lasts only 12 years instead of 28.5 years.
