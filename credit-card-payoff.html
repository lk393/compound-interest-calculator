❓ FAQ

▶
▶How much do I need to pay monthly to clear $5,000 credit card debt in 2 years at 18%?
You need to pay about $249 per month. Total interest would be $976, and total paid $5,976.
▶How long will it take to pay off a $5,000 balance if I pay $200/month at 18%?
It will take about 30 months (2.5 years). Total interest would be about $850.
▶What happens if I pay extra $50/month on my credit card?
On a $5,000 balance at 18%, adding $50/month reduces payoff time from 30 months to 25 months and saves about $200 in interest.
