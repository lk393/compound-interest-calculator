❓ FAQ

▶
▶ What is Coast FIRE?
Coast FIRE means you've saved enough that your current portfolio will grow to your FIRE Number by traditional retirement age — even if you never save another dollar. You can "coast" on your investments.
▶ Can I still save after Coast FIRE?
Absolutely. Reaching Coast FIRE doesn't mean you have to stop saving — it just means you have the flexibility to work less, switch careers, or spend more while still retiring on time.
▶ What if I'm already Coast FIRE?
Congrats! 🎉 You can relax your savings rate. Any extra savings will just get you to FIRE earlier or give you a bigger retirement buffer.
▶ Is the calculation accurate?
It's a solid estimate based on compound growth. Market returns vary — use a conservative return rate (like 6-7%) for more realistic planning.
