📖 How to read your results

▶

Down Payment Amount

The amount you'll need to pay upfront when purchasing the home. This is typically 20% of the purchase price for conventional loans.

Loan Amount

The amount you'll need to borrow from a lender. This is the purchase price minus your down payment.

Typical Requirements

Most conventional loans require 20% down to avoid private mortgage insurance (PMI). However, some programs allow as little as 3-5% down.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶Is a bigger down payment always better?
A larger down payment means a smaller loan, lower monthly payments, and less interest over time. However, you should also maintain an emergency fund — don't put every dollar into the down payment.
▶What is the minimum down payment?
For conventional loans, 20% is standard to avoid PMI. However, FHA loans can require as little as 3.5%, and some programs offer 0% down for qualified buyers.
