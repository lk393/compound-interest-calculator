📖 How to read your results

▶

CAGR

The steady annual growth rate that would produce the same final value over the investment period. A higher CAGR means better long-term performance.

Total Return

The percentage increase from your initial value to your final value over the entire period.

Total Profit

The dollar amount gained — Final Value minus Initial Value.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is the difference between CAGR and ROI?
ROI is the total return over the entire period. CAGR is the annualized return, which makes it easier to compare investments of different lengths.
▶What is a good CAGR?
For stock investments, a CAGR of 7-10% is considered solid for long-term investing. For bonds or savings accounts, 2-5% is typical.
