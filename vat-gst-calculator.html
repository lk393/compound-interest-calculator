❓ FAQ

▶
▶How much VAT is on a $500 purchase at 20% rate?
VAT = $500 × 0.20 = $100. Total price = $600. At 20% VAT, you pay $100 in tax on a $500 purchase.
▶What is the difference between VAT and GST?
VAT (Value Added Tax) and GST (Goods and Services Tax) are essentially the same — consumption taxes added at each stage of production. Rates vary by country: UK 20% VAT, Australia 10% GST, Canada 5% GST.
▶How do I calculate VAT from total including tax?
To find the pre-tax price: Pre-tax = Total ÷ (1 + VAT rate). For a $600 total at 20% VAT: pre-tax = $600 ÷ 1.20 = $500, VAT = $100.
