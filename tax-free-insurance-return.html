❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is the typical return of a tax-free insurance product?
Tax-free products like Roth IRAs and certain life insurance policies typically have an IRR of 4-6% over the long term. The tax-free advantage adds about 1-2% to your effective return.
▶Is a tax-free insurance product better than a taxable investment?
If a taxable investment returns 6% and you pay 20% tax, your after-tax return is 4.8%. A tax-free product returning 5% gives you 5% — a 0.2% advantage that compounds significantly over time.
▶Do I need to report tax-free insurance earnings?
In most cases, qualified distributions from Roth IRAs and certain life insurance policies are tax-free and do not need to be reported as taxable income. Check with a tax professional for your specific situation.
