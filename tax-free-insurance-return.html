❓ FAQ

▶
▶What types of insurance are tax‑free?
Roth IRAs, certain life insurance policies (like whole life), and some annuities offer tax‑free growth or tax‑free withdrawals. Check with your provider.
▶Is the tax advantage always worth it?
It depends on the return rate, fees, and your tax bracket. This tool helps you quantify the benefit. Always compare fees and surrender charges.
