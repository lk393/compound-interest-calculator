📖 How to read your results

▶

Effective Tax Rate

This is the percentage of your total income that goes to taxes. It's usually lower than your marginal tax rate.

Net Income

Your take-home pay after all taxes are deducted. This is the actual amount you have available for spending and saving.

Marginal vs. Effective

Your marginal rate is what you'd pay on the next dollar earned. Your effective rate is the average rate you pay on all your income.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What's the difference between effective and marginal tax rate?
Your marginal tax rate is the rate you pay on your last dollar of income. Your effective tax rate is the average rate you pay on all your income, which is usually lower.
▶How can I lower my effective tax rate?
You can lower your effective rate by using tax-advantaged accounts (like 401(k) or IRA), claiming deductions, using tax credits, or timing income and expenses strategically.
