❓ FAQ

▶
▶Is debt consolidation always a good idea?
Not always — it depends on the rates and fees. Consolidation is usually beneficial if you can get a significantly lower rate and avoid new debt.
▶What about consolidation fees?
This calculator does not include origination fees. Be sure to factor them in when evaluating a real consolidation offer.
