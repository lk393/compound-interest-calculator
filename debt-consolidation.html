❓ FAQ

▶
▶How much can I save by consolidating $15,000 of 22% credit card debt into a 10% personal loan?
You'd save about $4,500 in interest over 3 years. Monthly payment drops from $580 to $484 — a $96/month saving. Total savings with fees: about $3,500.
▶Is debt consolidation worth it?
It's worth it if you can get a rate 5%+ lower than current rates. On $15,000 over 3 years, a 10% consolidation loan saves $4,500 vs 22% credit cards. Factor in fees (2-5%) for true savings.
▶What credit score do I need for debt consolidation?
Generally 650+ for good rates. 700+ qualifies for the best rates (6-10%). Below 620, you may need a co-signer or secured loan.
