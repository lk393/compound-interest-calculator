Total Investment

Your total contributions over the entire period (monthly SIP × number of months).

Total Returns

The difference between your target amount and total investment — the profit earned through compounding.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is SIP?
A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is a method of investing a fixed amount regularly (monthly) in mutual funds, allowing you to benefit from rupee cost averaging and compounding.
▶Is this calculator accurate for real investments?
It provides an estimate based on a constant annual return. Real returns may vary due to market fluctuations, so use this as a planning guide.
