❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is a cost‑of‑living index?
It's a relative measure of the price of goods and services in a city compared to a base city (usually 100). Higher numbers mean higher cost.
▶How accurate is this?
It's a simplified estimate based on average indices. Actual costs vary by lifestyle and specific consumption patterns.
