❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is the cost of living difference between NYC and Bangkok?
NYC has a cost-of-living index of 100, Bangkok around 40. A $5,000 budget in NYC would be equivalent to about $2,000 in Bangkok — a 60% savings.
▶How do I compare living costs between cities?
Use cost-of-living indices. If City A index = 120 and City B index = 80, living in B is (120/80) = 1.5x cheaper. A $3,000 budget in A would be $2,000 in B.
▶What factors are included in cost-of-living indices?
Typically include housing, food, transportation, utilities, healthcare, and entertainment. Rent often accounts for 30-40% of the index.
