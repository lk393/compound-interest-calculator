❓ FAQ

▶
▶How do I calculate prorated salary if I start a $60,000 job mid-month?
For a $60,000 salary, daily rate is $230.77 (based on 260 working days). If you start on the 15th with 10 working days left, your prorated salary would be $2,307.70.
▶How do I calculate salary for a partial month?
Daily rate = annual salary ÷ 260 working days. Prorated amount = daily rate × number of working days. For a $60,000 salary and 10 days worked: $230.77 × 10 = $2,307.70.
▶What is the difference between 30-day and working-day proration?
30-day proration divides salary by 30 (or 365). Working-day proration divides by 260 (business days). For a $60,000 salary and 10 working days, 30-day proration gives $1,666.67, while working-day proration gives $2,307.70 — a $641 difference.
