❓ FAQ

▶
▶ How does an extra payment save me money?
Every extra dollar you pay reduces your principal balance. That means less interest accrues on the remaining balance — saving you money and shortening your loan term.
▶ Is it always worth making extra payments?
Usually yes, if your mortgage rate is above 4-5%. But consider your other financial priorities — high-interest debt or emergency savings should come first.
▶ Can I make extra payments at any time?
Yes — most mortgages allow extra payments without penalty. However, some loans have prepayment penalties, so check your loan terms first.
▶ Does the calculation assume I keep paying extra every month?
Yes — it assumes you make the same extra payment consistently each month until the loan is paid off. If you stop early, the savings will be less.
▶ What's the minimum extra payment I should make?
Even $50/month can make a meaningful difference over 30 years. Use this calculator to see exactly how much — you might be surprised!
