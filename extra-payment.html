❓ FAQ

▶
▶How much can I save by paying an extra $100/month on a $200,000 mortgage at 6.5%?
Adding $100/month to your payment saves about $28,000 in interest and pays off the loan 3.5 years early on a 30-year mortgage.
▶How much extra should I pay to cut my 30-year mortgage to 15 years?
On a $200,000 loan at 6.5%, you'd need to pay about $600 extra each month to halve the term from 30 to 15 years. Total interest saved would be over $110,000.
▶Is it better to make extra mortgage payments or invest the money?
If your mortgage rate is 6.5%, paying extra gives a guaranteed 6.5% return. If you can earn 8%+ investing, investing may be better. Compare after-tax returns.
