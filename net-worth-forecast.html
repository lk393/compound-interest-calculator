❓ FAQ

▶
▶If I have $50,000 net worth and save $1,000/month, what will I have in 20 years?
At 7% annual return, $50,000 growing with $1,000/month savings will reach approximately $712,000 in 20 years.
▶How long will it take to reach a $1 million net worth?
Starting from $0 at 7% return, saving $2,000/month would take about 22 years to reach $1 million. Saving $3,000/month would take about 17 years.
▶What is a good net worth by age?
A common rule is to have your age × annual salary / 10 as a net worth target. By 40, you should have 3-4× your annual salary; by 50, 6-7×.
