❓ FAQ

▶
▶ What is net worth?
Net worth is the total value of everything you own (assets) minus everything you owe (liabilities). It's the most comprehensive measure of your financial health.
▶ What growth rate should I use?
For long‑term projections, 6‑8% is common for a diversified stock portfolio. Use a more conservative rate (4‑5%) if you include bonds or cash. Adjust based on your actual asset allocation.
▶ How accurate is this forecast?
This is a simplified projection based on constant returns and savings. Real returns fluctuate, and life circumstances change. Use this as a planning tool, not a guarantee.
