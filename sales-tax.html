❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is the typical sales tax rate?
Sales tax rates vary by state and country. In the US, state rates range from 0% to 10%, with local additions in some areas.
▶Is sales tax the same everywhere?
No — rates vary by jurisdiction and some items (like groceries or clothing) may be exempt from sales tax in certain locations.
