❓ FAQ

▶
▶How much can I save by refinancing a $200,000 mortgage from 7% to 6%?
On a $200,000 30-year mortgage, reducing your rate from 7% to 6% lowers your monthly payment from $1,331 to $1,199 — a $132/month savings. Over the life of the loan, you save about $31,000 in interest, minus closing costs.
▶What is the break-even point for refinancing?
If closing costs are $5,000 and you save $132/month, your break-even is 38 months ($5,000 ÷ $132). You need to stay in the home for at least 38 months to come out ahead.
▶Is it worth refinancing for a 0.5% rate drop?
On a $200,000 loan, a 0.5% drop from 7% to 6.5% saves about $65/month. With $5,000 closing costs, the break-even is 77 months (6.4 years). Only refinance if you plan to stay that long.
▶How much does refinancing cost?
Typical refinancing costs are 2-5% of the loan amount. On a $200,000 loan, that's $4,000-$10,000. Common fees include appraisal ($500), title search ($1,000), origination fee (1%), and recording fees.
