❓ FAQ

▶
▶Should I always choose the lower interest rate?
Not necessarily — a lower rate with a longer term can sometimes cost more in total interest than a higher rate with a shorter term. This calculator helps you compare the total cost of each option.
▶What about fees and closing costs?
This calculator compares the principal, interest, and loan terms only. For a complete comparison, be sure to factor in origination fees, closing costs, and any other charges separately.
