❓ FAQ

▶
▶How much can I save by choosing a 15-year vs 30-year mortgage on $200,000 at 6.5%?
30-year: $1,264/mo, total interest $255,000. 15-year: $1,743/mo, total interest $113,700. You save $141,300 in interest but pay $479 more per month.
▶Which loan is better: a 5% loan with $2,000 fees or a 5.5% loan with no fees?
For a $200,000 30-year loan, the 5% loan saves $20,000 in interest but costs $2,000 fees. Net savings $18,000 — the 5% loan is still better despite fees.
▶What's the difference between APR and interest rate?
The interest rate is the cost of borrowing money. APR includes fees and other costs, giving you the true cost of the loan. A 6% loan with $5,000 fees might have a 6.5% APR.
