❓ FAQ

▶
▶ What is amortization?
Amortization is the process of paying off a loan over time through regular payments. Each payment covers both interest and principal, with the balance gradually decreasing to zero.
▶ Why do I pay more interest at the beginning?
Because interest is calculated on your remaining balance. Early on, the balance is high, so interest is high. As you pay down principal, interest decreases.
▶ What if I make extra payments?
Extra payments reduce your principal faster, which saves on interest and shortens your loan term. This calculator shows the standard schedule without extra payments.
▶ Can I use this for any type of loan?
Yes — mortgages, car loans, personal loans, student loans. Any fixed-rate loan with regular payments can be amortized using this calculator.
