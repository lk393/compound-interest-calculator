❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is amortization?
Amortization is the process of paying off a loan over time through regular payments. Each payment covers both interest and principal.
▶Why do I pay more interest at the beginning?
Because interest is calculated on your remaining balance. Early on, the balance is high, so interest is high.
