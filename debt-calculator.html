❓ FAQ

▶
▶ Snowball vs. Avalanche – which is better?
Snowball targets the smallest balance first (motivating), Avalanche targets the highest rate first (saves more interest). Choose what fits your personality.
▶ What is the minimum payment?
The smallest amount your lender requires each month. You must pay at least this to avoid penalties.
▶ How does extra payment help?
Every extra dollar you pay reduces the principal faster, cutting down both interest and the total time to get out of debt.
▶ Is this calculator accurate?
It uses standard compound interest formulas and assumes payments are made monthly. It's a great planning tool, but actual lender terms may vary.
