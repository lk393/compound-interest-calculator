❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is an amortizing loan?
An amortizing loan has fixed monthly payments where each payment covers both interest and principal. Early payments go mostly toward interest; later payments go mostly toward principal.
▶How can I reduce the total interest I pay?
You can reduce total interest by choosing a shorter loan term, shopping for a lower interest rate, making a larger down payment, or making extra principal payments early in the loan term.
