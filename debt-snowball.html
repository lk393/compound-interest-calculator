❓ FAQ

▶
▶Which is better: debt snowball or avalanche method?
Avalanche saves more money ($500-$2,000) by targeting highest interest first. Snowball gives psychological wins by paying smallest balances first. Both work — choose what keeps you motivated.
▶How much can I save using avalanche vs snowball on $15,000 debt?
With three debts at 8%, 15%, and 22% interest, avalanche saves about $1,200 in interest and pays off 3 months faster than snowball on a $15,000 total balance.
▶What is the debt snowball method?
Pay minimums on all debts, then put extra money toward the smallest balance first. Once paid off, roll that payment to the next smallest. Dave Ramsey popularized this method.
