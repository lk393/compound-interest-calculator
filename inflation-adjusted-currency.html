❓ FAQ

▶
▶What would $100 from 1990 be worth today at 3% inflation?
$100 × (1.03)^35 = $281. Today, $100 from 1990 would be worth $281 in nominal dollars, but its purchasing power is the same as $100 in 1990.
▶How much will $50,000 be worth in 20 years at 3% inflation?
$50,000 × (1.03)^20 = $90,306. In 20 years, $50,000 will need to be $90,306 just to have the same purchasing power as today.
▶What is the historical average inflation rate?
The historical average inflation rate in the US is about 3.2% per year (1913-2025). Since 2000, it has averaged about 2.5% per year.
