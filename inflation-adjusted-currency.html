📖 How to read your results

▶

Adjusted Amount

What the past amount would be worth today, after accounting for inflation. It reflects the same purchasing power.

Total Inflation

The cumulative percentage increase in prices over the period.

Purchasing Power Lost

The percentage of purchasing power that has been eroded by inflation. A higher number means more value loss.

❓ FAQ

▶What is the average inflation rate used?
The default is 3%, a common long‑term average. You can adjust it based on historical data for your specific country.
▶Why is this useful?
It helps you understand the real value of historical prices, salaries, or savings. For example, $100 from 1980 is worth much less today.
