📖 How to read your results

▶

Time to Pay Off

The number of months (and years) it will take to completely pay off your debt with your current payment plan.

Total Interest

The total amount of interest you will pay by the time the debt is fully paid off.

Total Paid

The total amount you will pay (principal + interest) over the entire repayment period.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What if I can't pay the full balance each month?
This calculator assumes you make a fixed monthly payment. If you can't afford the minimum payment, consider reducing your payment amount or extending the term — but this will increase total interest.
▶How can I pay off debt faster?
Increase your monthly payment, pay extra toward principal, or reduce your interest rate through balance transfer or refinancing.
