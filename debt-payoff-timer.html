❓ FAQ

▶
▶When will I be debt-free if I pay $500/month on $10,000 at 15%?
You will be debt-free in 24 months (2 years). You'll pay about $1,200 in total interest.
▶How much extra should I pay to become debt-free in 1 year on $10,000 at 15%?
You need to pay about $903/month. That's $403/month extra compared to the 24-month $500 payment. You'd save about $600 in interest.
