❓ FAQ

▶
▶How do I calculate NPV for a project with $50,000 initial cost and $15,000 annual cash flow for 5 years?
At 10% discount rate, NPV = -$50,000 + $15,000 × [1 - (1.10)^-5] / 0.10 = $6,861. Positive NPV means the project is worthwhile.
▶What does a positive NPV mean?
A positive NPV means the investment is expected to generate more value than its cost. A $10,000 positive NPV means the project is expected to create $10,000 in value beyond the required return.
▶What discount rate should I use for NPV?
Use your company's WACC (Weighted Average Cost of Capital). For individuals, use your required rate of return (typically 8-12% for stocks, 15-20% for startups).
