📖 How to read your results

▶

Net Present Value (NPV)

The present value of all future cash flows minus the initial investment. Positive NPV = profitable investment.

Total Cash Flows

The sum of all future cash flows (not discounted).

Profit / Loss

The difference between total cash flows and the initial investment (un-discounted).

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is NPV used for?
NPV is used in capital budgeting to evaluate the profitability of an investment or project. It accounts for the time value of money.
▶What discount rate should I use?
The discount rate should reflect your cost of capital or the required rate of return. A common choice is 8-12% for many business investments.
