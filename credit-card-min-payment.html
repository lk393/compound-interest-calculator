❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is the typical minimum payment?
Most credit cards require a minimum payment of 1-3% of the outstanding balance, with a minimum of $25-35.
▶Why does it take so long to pay off with minimum payments?
Because most of your payment goes toward interest, especially early on. The principal balance drops very slowly, extending the payoff period dramatically.
