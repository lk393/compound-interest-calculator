❓ FAQ

▶
▶Is it better to buy a $300,000 home with cash or a mortgage at 6.5%?
With a mortgage at 6.5%, you'd pay about $382,633 in total interest over 30 years. If you can earn 7%+ on investments, financing may be better. Cash saves $382,633 in interest but ties up $300,000.
▶What is the opportunity cost of paying cash for a home?
Paying cash ties up $300,000 that could earn $65,000-$100,000 over 5 years at 4-6% returns. The opportunity cost is the investment income you forgo.
▶At what mortgage rate does it make sense to pay cash instead of financing?
If mortgage rates are above 7% and you can't earn more investing, paying cash is better. At 8%, a $300,000 mortgage costs $490,000 in interest — cash becomes more attractive.
