📖 How to read your results

▶

Mortgage Interest

Total interest paid over the loan term. This is the cost of borrowing.

Opportunity Cost

If you pay cash, you forgo the investment returns you could have earned on that money.

Net Wealth

Home value minus total cash spent (including interest or lost returns). A positive difference means mortgage is better.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶Is it better to pay cash or get a mortgage?
It depends on the mortgage rate, your investment return rate, and your risk tolerance. If you can earn more investing than the mortgage rate, a mortgage may be better.
▶Why include home appreciation?
Appreciation affects the final value of the home, which is part of your net wealth in both scenarios. It's usually the same whether you use cash or mortgage.
