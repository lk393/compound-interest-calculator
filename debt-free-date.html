❓ FAQ

▶
▶When will I be debt-free if I pay $500/month on $10,000 at 12%?
You will be debt-free in 23 months (1 year 11 months). The debt-free date would be July 2028 if you start today.
▶What is the exact date I'll be debt-free?
Enter your balance, rate, and monthly payment. The calculator gives you the exact month and year you'll make your final payment.
▶What if I increase my payment to $600/month on $10,000 at 12%?
You would be debt-free in 18 months (1.5 years) instead of 23 months — 5 months earlier. You'd save about $400 in interest.
