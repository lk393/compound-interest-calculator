📖 How to read your results

▶

Emergency Fund Goal

Your target savings: monthly expenses × months of coverage. Typically 3‑6 months for most people.

Shortfall

The difference between your goal and current savings. This is how much more you need to save.

Time to Goal

How many months it will take to reach your goal at your current savings rate.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶How many months should I cover?
3‑6 months is standard. If you have variable income or a family, consider 6‑12 months.
▶Where should I keep my emergency fund?
In a high‑yield savings account or money market fund — easily accessible but still earning some interest.
