❓ FAQ

▶
▶How much is $1,000 USD in EUR at 0.92 exchange rate?
$1,000 × 0.92 = €920. At a 0.92 exchange rate, $1,000 USD equals €920. Always check the live mid-market rate for accuracy.
▶What is the difference between mid-market rate and bank rate?
The mid-market rate is the real exchange rate between currencies. Banks add a markup (typically 2-5%) to make a profit. A 1.20 mid-market rate might be offered at 1.17 by a bank — costing you $25 more on a $1,000 transfer.
▶How do I get the best exchange rate when traveling?
Use a credit card with no foreign transaction fees and withdraw cash in local currency from ATMs. Avoid airport kiosks — they can charge 10-15% above the mid-market rate.
