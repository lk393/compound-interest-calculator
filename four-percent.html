❓ FAQ

▶
▶ What is the 4% rule?
The 4% rule says you can withdraw 4% of your portfolio in the first year of retirement, then adjust for inflation each year. It's designed to last 30+ years based on historical market data.
▶ Where does the 25x rule come from?
25x is just another way of saying the 4% rule. If you need $40,000/year, multiply by 25 = $1,000,000. 4% of $1,000,000 = $40,000.
▶ Is the 4% rule still safe?
Many still use it as a rule of thumb, but some argue 3-3.5% is safer for longer retirements (40+ years). Use a lower rate if you're retiring early.
▶ Does this include inflation?
Yes — the 4% rule was designed for a 30-year retirement with inflation adjustments. You increase your withdrawal amount each year with inflation.
▶ Can I withdraw more than 4%?
You could, but it increases the risk of running out of money. A higher withdrawal rate means a shorter "safe" retirement period or requires more flexibility in your spending.
