❓ FAQ

▶
▶How much long-term care insurance coverage do I need?
Cover 3-5 years of care. With monthly care costs averaging $5,000-$8,000, a 3-year policy would need $180,000-$288,000 in coverage.
▶What is the average cost of long-term care insurance?
For a 50-year-old, a $150,000 benefit policy costs about $2,000-$3,500/year. Premiums increase with age — a 65-year-old could pay $4,000-$6,000/year.
▶When is the best time to buy long-term care insurance?
The optimal age is 45-55. A 50-year-old pays about $2,500/year vs $6,000/year at 65. Buying early locks in lower rates and ensures insurability.
