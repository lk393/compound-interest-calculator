Elimination Period

Number of days you must pay for care yourself before the policy starts paying. Longer periods lower premiums.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What does long‑term care insurance cover?
It covers care in a nursing home, assisted living, or home care for chronic illnesses, disabilities, or cognitive disorders like Alzheimer's.
▶When should I buy LTC insurance?
The best time is typically in your 50s or early 60s when premiums are lower and you're still healthy enough to qualify.
