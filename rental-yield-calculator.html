📖 How to read your results

▶

Gross Rental Yield

Annual rent divided by property value. This is the return before any expenses — a quick snapshot of your potential return.

Net Rental Yield

Annual rent minus holding costs, divided by property value. This reflects your true return after expenses.

Typical Range

Yields vary by location and property type, typically between 2% and 8%. Higher net yields indicate better investment returns.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is considered a good rental yield?
A good net rental yield is typically 4% or higher. However, this depends on local market conditions, property type, interest rates, and your investment goals.
▶Why is net yield lower than gross yield?
Net yield subtracts holding costs like property taxes, insurance, maintenance, and property management fees. These costs typically reduce the yield by 0.5% to 2%.
