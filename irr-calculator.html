❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is the IRR of a project that costs $50,000 and returns $15,000/year for 5 years?
The IRR is approximately 15.2%. Since this exceeds a 10% cost of capital, the project is financially viable.
▶What is the difference between IRR and ROI?
ROI is a simple percentage return on investment. IRR is an annualized rate of return. A 50% ROI over 5 years equals an IRR of about 8.4% per year.
▶What is a good IRR for a startup investment?
Startups typically require 20-30% IRR to compensate for higher risk. Established businesses target 10-15%. A 25% IRR means the investment is expected to return 25% per year.
