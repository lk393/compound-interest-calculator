📖 How to read your results

▶

IRR

The annualized return rate that makes the net present value of all cash flows equal to zero. Higher IRR indicates a more profitable investment.

Total Cash Flow

The sum of all cash flows (including the initial investment).

Net Profit

The total profit over the investment period — total cash flow minus initial investment.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is a good IRR?
A good IRR depends on your cost of capital. Generally, an IRR higher than your required rate of return (e.g., 10-15%) indicates a profitable investment.
▶What's the difference between IRR and ROI?
ROI is the total return over the entire period. IRR is the annualized return that accounts for the timing of cash flows.
