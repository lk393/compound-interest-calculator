❓ FAQ

▶
▶How much does car insurance cost for a $25,000 vehicle with a clean record?
On average, $1,200-$1,800 per year for full coverage. Your rate depends on your location, age, and driving history.
▶Why is car insurance more expensive for new cars?
New cars have higher replacement costs and repair costs. A $30,000 new car costs about $1,500-$2,000/year to insure, vs $800-$1,200 for a 5-year-old car.
▶How much does a speeding ticket increase insurance rates?
A single speeding ticket can increase your premium by 20-30% on average. A $1,500 premium could increase to $1,800-$1,950 for 3-5 years.
