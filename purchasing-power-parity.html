📖 How to read your results

▶

PPP Exchange Rate

The rate that equalizes the price of the basket in both countries. If different from the nominal rate, it indicates over/under valuation.

Adjusted Amount

What your amount is worth in the foreign country in local currency terms based on PPP.

Valuation

If the PPP rate is higher than the nominal rate, the home currency is undervalued (and vice versa).

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is PPP?
Purchasing Power Parity is an economic theory that compares currencies through a basket of goods. It suggests exchange rates should adjust to equalize the price of identical goods in different countries.
▶Why is PPP useful?
It helps compare living standards, cost of living, and economic productivity between countries without the noise of volatile nominal exchange rates.
