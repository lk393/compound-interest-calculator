❓ FAQ

▶
▶How much health insurance coverage do I need with a $100,000 household income?
Recommended coverage is 5-10x your annual income, or $500,000-$1,000,000. If you have a family history of chronic conditions, consider $1.5 million.
▶What is the difference between health insurance and critical illness insurance?
Health insurance (medical insurance) reimburses treatment costs. Critical illness insurance pays a lump sum upon diagnosis to cover income loss and out-of-pocket costs.
▶How much health insurance should I buy for my child?
For children, $150,000-$300,000 coverage is recommended to cover serious conditions like leukemia, where treatment can cost $200,000+.
