📖 How to read your results

▶

Final Value

The total value of your portfolio at the end of the investment period, including all contributions and compounded returns.

Total Returns

The total profit earned on your investments. Final Value minus Total Invested.

Return on Investment (ROI)

The percentage return on your total contributions. A higher ROI means your money worked harder for you.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶ What is a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP)?
SIP is a method of investing a fixed amount regularly (usually monthly) into mutual funds or ETFs. It helps you build wealth through rupee-cost averaging and the power of compounding.
▶ What's a realistic expected return for SIP?
For equity mutual funds, a long-term average of 10-14% is common. For conservative estimates, use 10-12%. For debt funds or bonds, use 6-8%.
▶ What if I stop my SIP early?
You can stop anytime, but remember — compounding needs time to work its magic. The longer you stay invested, the more powerful the effect.
▶ Is SIP only for mutual funds?
No — the SIP concept applies to any regular investment: ETFs, stocks, cryptocurrencies, or even a personal savings plan. It's about consistency.
▶ What's the minimum amount for SIP?
In many countries, you can start a mutual fund SIP with as little as $20-$50 per month. Check with your local provider for exact limits.
