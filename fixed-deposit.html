❓ FAQ

▶
▶How much will I earn on a $10,000 fixed deposit at 5% for 3 years?
At 5% compound interest, your $10,000 FD will grow to approximately $11,576 in 3 years, earning you $1,576 in interest.
▶Is it better to invest in FD or savings account?
FDs typically offer 4-6% returns, while savings accounts offer only 1-2%. A $10,000 FD at 5% for 5 years earns $2,762, compared to just $500-$1,000 in a savings account.
▶What happens if I withdraw my FD before maturity?
Early withdrawal usually incurs a penalty of 0.5-1% reduction in interest rate. On a 3-year FD, withdrawing early could reduce your returns by 20-30%.
