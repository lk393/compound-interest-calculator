📖 How to read your results

▶

Maturity Value

The total amount you will receive at maturity — your deposit plus all compounded interest.

Total Interest Earned

The total interest earned over the entire tenure. This is the difference between maturity value and your deposit.

Effective Annual Rate (EAR)

The actual annual return accounting for compounding frequency. Higher compounding frequency = higher EAR.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is a fixed deposit?
A fixed deposit (also called a term deposit or time deposit) is a savings account where you deposit a lump sum for a fixed period at a guaranteed interest rate.
▶Is the interest guaranteed?
Yes — fixed deposits typically offer a guaranteed fixed interest rate for the entire tenure, making them one of the safest investment options.
