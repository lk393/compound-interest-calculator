📖 How to read your results

▶

Projected Retirement Age

The age at which you will have enough savings to cover your retirement spending using the 4% rule.

Target FIRE Number

Your target retirement number — 25 times your annual retirement spending (based on the 4% rule).

Years Until Retirement

The number of years from your current age until your projected retirement age.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is the 4% rule used here?
The 4% rule says you can withdraw 4% of your portfolio in the first year of retirement, adjusting for inflation each year. So your FIRE number = Annual Spending × 25.
▶What if I want to retire earlier?
Increase your monthly contributions, earn higher returns, or reduce your retirement spending target. Each extra dollar saved brings retirement closer.
