📖 How to read your results

▶

Final Balance

Your total account value at the end. Includes principal, all contributions, and compounded interest.

Total Interest

All the interest you earned. Final balance minus what you actually deposited (principal + contributions).

Effective Annual Rate

Your true yearly return after compounding. More frequent compounding means this number is slightly higher than the nominal rate.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶ What is compound interest?
Interest on interest. Your earnings generate more earnings. The longer the time, the bigger the effect.
▶ What's the formula?
Don't worry about it — the calculator does the math. Just enter your numbers and you're good.
▶ Nominal rate vs. effective annual rate?
Nominal is the advertised rate. Effective annual rate is what you actually earn after compounding. For example, 7% compounded monthly gives you 7.23%.
▶ Does compounding frequency matter?
Yes, but not huge in the short term. $10k over 10 years: annual vs. monthly compounding — a few hundred bucks difference. The gap grows over time.
▶ How much does monthly contribution matter?
A lot. $10k with no extra contributions → ~$20k after 10 years. Add $500/month → ~$106k. That's the power of regular investing.
