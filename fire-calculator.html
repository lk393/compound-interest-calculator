❓ FAQ

▶
▶ What is the 4% rule?
The 4% rule says you can withdraw 4% of your portfolio each year without running out of money. So your FIRE Number = Annual Spending × 25.
▶ How accurate is the Years to FIRE estimate?
It's a good estimate assuming steady returns and consistent savings. Market returns vary, so treat it as a planning guide, not a guarantee.
▶ What's a realistic expected return?
Many use 6-8% for stock-heavy portfolios. 7% is a common historical average after inflation. Use a conservative number for safer planning.
▶ Can I retire earlier than the estimate?
Yes — increase your savings rate, earn higher returns, or reduce your annual spending. Every extra dollar saved brings FIRE closer.
▶ Does this include inflation?
The 4% rule already accounts for inflation. The estimate assumes your spending grows with inflation and your returns are above inflation.
