❓ FAQ

▶
▶How much do I need to retire early if I spend $40,000 per year?
Using the 4% rule, you need $1,000,000 ($40,000 × 25). Using the 3.5% rule (more conservative for early retirement), you need about $1,143,000.
▶If I save $2,000/month at 7% return, how long to reach $1 million?
Starting from $0, saving $2,000/month at 7% annual return would take about 21.5 years to reach $1 million.
▶What is the 4% rule for FIRE?
The 4% rule means you can withdraw 4% of your portfolio in the first year of retirement, then adjust for inflation. A portfolio of $1 million would give you $40,000/year in retirement income.
▶What is the average age for FIRE?
The average FIRE retiree is between 35-45 years old. Some leanFIRE practitioners retire in their 30s, while regular FIRE practitioners typically retire in their 40s.
