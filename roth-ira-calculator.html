▶How much will a Roth IRA grow with $6,000/year for 30 years at 7%?
Your $180,000 in contributions would grow to approximately $566,764. All growth is tax-free. At 7% return, your account would earn $386,764 in tax-free returns.
▶What is the Roth IRA contribution limit for 2026?
The 2026 Roth IRA contribution limit is $7,000 ($8,000 if age 50+). If you contribute $7,000/year for 30 years at 7%, your balance would grow to about $661,225.
▶What is the difference between Roth IRA and Traditional IRA?
Roth IRA: contributions are after-tax, growth is tax-free, and withdrawals are tax-free. Traditional IRA: contributions are tax-deductible, growth is tax-deferred, but withdrawals are taxed as ordinary income.
▶How much tax do I save with a Roth IRA?
On $6,000/year for 30 years at 7%, you save about $386,764 in taxes on the growth compared to a taxable account, assuming a 22% tax rate on earnings.
