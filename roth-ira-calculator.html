❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is a Roth IRA?
A Roth IRA is a retirement account where you contribute after-tax money, and all growth and withdrawals are tax-free in retirement.
▶What are the contribution limits?
For 2026, the Roth IRA contribution limit is $7,000 ($8,000 if age 50 or older). This calculator uses a default of $6,500, adjust as needed.
