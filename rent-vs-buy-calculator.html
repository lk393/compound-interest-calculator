❓ FAQ

▶
▶Is it cheaper to rent or buy a $300,000 home with 20% down and $1,800 rent?
At 6.5% interest, monthly mortgage is about $1,896. With taxes and insurance, total monthly is ~$2,400 vs $1,800 rent. Buying costs $600/month more, but you build equity. Break-even is about 5-7 years.
▶How many years do I need to live in a home for buying to be better than renting?
The break-even point is typically 5-7 years. For a $300,000 home at 6.5% with $1,800 rent, you'd need to stay about 6 years to recoup closing costs and transaction fees.
▶What costs should I include when comparing rent vs buy?
Include: rent/renters insurance vs mortgage, property taxes, homeowners insurance, HOA fees, maintenance (1% of home value annually), and closing costs (2-5% of purchase price).
