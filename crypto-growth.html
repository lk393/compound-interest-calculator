❓ FAQ

▶What is a realistic crypto return?
Historically, Bitcoin and major cryptocurrencies have seen 10-20% annualized returns, but with much higher volatility. Use a conservative estimate (10-15%) for long-term planning.
▶Does this account for market volatility?
No — this assumes a steady annual return. Crypto markets are highly volatile. This projection is a planning tool, not a guarantee.
