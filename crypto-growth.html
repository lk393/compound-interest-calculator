❓ FAQ

▶What would $5,000 in Bitcoin be worth in 10 years at 30% annual growth?
$5,000 at 30% annual growth for 10 years would grow to approximately $68,930. That's a total return of 1,278%.
▶What is a realistic growth rate for cryptocurrency investments?
Bitcoin's historical CAGR is about 100%+ over 10+ years, but this is volatile. A realistic long-term rate for crypto is 20-40% per year, compared to 7-10% for stocks.
▶How does dollar-cost averaging work in crypto?
DCA means investing a fixed amount regularly (e.g., $100/week). This reduces the impact of volatility. In a volatile market, DCA can lower your average purchase price by 15-30%.
