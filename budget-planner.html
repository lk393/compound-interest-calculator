❓ FAQ

▶
▶How much should I spend on needs with a $5,000 monthly income?
With a $5,000 monthly income, the 50/30/20 rule suggests $2,500 (50%) for needs, $1,500 (30%) for wants, and $1,000 (20%) for savings and debt repayment.
▶What counts as a 'need' vs a 'want' in the 50/30/20 rule?
Needs: housing, utilities, groceries, insurance, minimum debt payments. Wants: dining out, entertainment, streaming services, shopping, vacations. Savings: retirement, emergency fund, extra debt payments.
▶Is the 50/30/20 rule realistic for high-cost cities?
In high-cost cities, needs often exceed 50%. A modified rule like 60/20/20 or 70/15/15 may be more realistic. A $5,000 income in NYC might spend $3,000 on housing alone, leaving less for wants and savings.
