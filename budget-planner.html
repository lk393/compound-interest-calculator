❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is the 50/30/20 rule?
It's a simple budgeting framework where 50% of your after-tax income goes to Needs, 30% to Wants, and 20% to Savings and debt repayment.
▶What if my needs exceed 50%?
Adjust the percentages to fit your situation. The 50/30/20 rule is a guideline — you can modify it based on your cost of living and financial goals.
