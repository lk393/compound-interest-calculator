❓ FAQ

▶
▶How much should I have in my emergency fund for a $5,000 monthly expense?
For 3 months: $15,000. For 6 months: $30,000. The recommended amount is 3-6 months of essential expenses. For most people with $5,000 monthly expenses, $15,000-$30,000 is the target.
▶How much emergency fund do I need if I have a family?
With a family and children, 6-9 months is often recommended. For a family with $6,000 monthly expenses, a 6-month fund would be $36,000, and a 9-month fund would be $54,000.
▶Where should I keep my emergency fund?
A high-yield savings account earning 4-5% APY is ideal. It should be easily accessible but separate from your checking account to avoid spending it on non-emergencies.
