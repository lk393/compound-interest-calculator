❓ FAQ

▶
▶How much tax will I pay on $100,000 foreign income with 20% foreign tax and 24% US rate?
Foreign tax paid = $20,000. US tax before credit = $24,000. Foreign tax credit = $20,000. Net US tax = $4,000. Total tax = $24,000 — same as if earned in the US.
▶What is the foreign tax credit?
The foreign tax credit allows you to deduct taxes paid to foreign countries from your US tax bill. It prevents double taxation. You can claim a credit up to the US tax on that foreign income.
▶Do I have to pay US tax on foreign income?
Yes, US citizens must report and pay tax on worldwide income. However, you can use the Foreign Tax Credit or Foreign Earned Income Exclusion ($120,000 for 2026) to reduce or eliminate US tax.
