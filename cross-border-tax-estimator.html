📖 How to read your results

▶

Foreign Tax

Tax paid to the foreign country on your income there.

Foreign Tax Credit

The portion of foreign tax that can be claimed as a credit against your home country tax, preventing double taxation.

Total Tax

The sum of foreign tax and any remaining home tax after credit. This is your total tax burden on foreign income.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is the foreign tax credit?
It's a credit allowed by many countries to reduce double taxation on foreign income. You can claim a credit for taxes paid to a foreign country against your home tax liability.
▶Is this calculator accurate for all countries?
No, it's a simplified estimate. Tax treaties, deductions, and specific rules vary greatly. Consult a tax professional for precise advice.
