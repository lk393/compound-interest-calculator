❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is backtesting and why is it important?
Backtesting tests a strategy on historical data. If a strategy performed well in the past, it's more likely to succeed in the future. A strategy that returns 10%+ with low drawdown is considered good.
▶How do I backtest a simple buy-and-hold strategy?
Enter the investment amount, years, and expected return. The tool shows the final value. A $10,000 investment at 7% for 20 years grows to $38,697.
▶What is a good Sharpe ratio for backtesting?
A Sharpe ratio above 1.0 is good, above 2.0 is excellent. It measures risk-adjusted returns: returns per unit of risk. A 12% return with 10% volatility has a Sharpe ratio of 1.0.
