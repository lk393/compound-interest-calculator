📖 How to read your results

▶

Option Value

Estimated fair value using a simplified Black‑Scholes model. This is what the option should be worth.

Intrinsic Value

The difference between stock price and strike price (if positive). The "in‑the‑money" amount.

Time Value

The portion of the option value above intrinsic value — the "insurance" value of the option.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is an employee stock option?
It gives you the right to buy company stock at a fixed price (strike) for a limited time. You profit if the stock price rises above the strike.
▶What does "moneyness" mean?
"In‑the‑money" means the stock price is above the strike price. "Out‑of‑the‑money" means it's below.
