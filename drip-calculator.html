❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is DRIP?
DRIP stands for Dividend Reinvestment Plan. It's a strategy where you automatically use dividend payments to purchase more shares of the same stock instead of taking cash.
▶Why is DRIP powerful?
DRIP harnesses the power of compounding — your dividends buy more shares, which generate more dividends, creating a snowball effect that accelerates your returns over time.
