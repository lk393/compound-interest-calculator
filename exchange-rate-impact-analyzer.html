📖 How to read your results

▶

Gain/Loss

The difference in home‑currency value due to the exchange rate change. A positive number is a gain.

Percentage Change

The relative change in home‑currency value.

Direction

If the new rate is higher (you get more foreign currency per home currency), your foreign assets are worth less in home currency.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶Who uses this tool?
Investors with foreign assets, international businesses, and anyone with exposure to foreign currencies.
▶Can it predict future rates?
No, it only calculates the impact of a specified rate change. It does not forecast exchange rates.
