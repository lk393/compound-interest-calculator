❓ FAQ

▶
▶Is it better to invest pre-tax or post-tax?
If you expect to be in a lower tax bracket in retirement, pre-tax (Traditional 401k) is better. If you expect higher taxes, post-tax (Roth) is better. On $10,000 invested, pre-tax saves $2,200 now, but Roth saves taxes on $40,000+ of growth.
▶How do pre-tax and post-tax investments compare after 30 years?
For a $10,000 investment at 7% for 30 years at 22% tax rate: Pre-tax = $76,123 before tax, $59,376 after tax. Post-tax = $7,800 invested, grows to $59,376 (tax-free). They're equal if tax rates stay the same.
▶What is the advantage of a Roth IRA?
Roth IRA contributions are post-tax, but all growth and withdrawals are tax-free. On $6,500/year for 30 years at 7%, a Roth saves about $100,000 in taxes compared to a taxable account.
