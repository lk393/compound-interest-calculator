❓ FAQ

▶
▶Does investing weekly instead of monthly improve returns?
Weekly investing gives a slight advantage. On $1,000/month at 12% for 10 years, monthly SIP gives $230,038. Weekly SIP gives $232,137 — a $2,099 difference (0.9% more).
▶What is the difference between monthly and daily SIP?
Daily SIP invests the same amount divided by 30 each day. On $1,000/month at 12% for 10 years, daily SIP gives $232,560 vs $230,038 for monthly — a $2,522 difference. The earlier money is invested, the more it compounds.
▶Which SIP frequency is best for long-term investors?
Daily or weekly SIPs give slightly better returns due to earlier investment. The difference is small (0.5-1.5% over 10 years). Choose a frequency that aligns with your cash flow (e.g., monthly for salary earners).
