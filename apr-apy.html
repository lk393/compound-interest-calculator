The total annual cost of borrowing, including interest plus fees. This is what you actually pay.

The actual annual interest rate after compounding, without fees.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What's the difference between APR and APY?
APR is the annual cost of borrowing (interest + fees). APY is the annual return on savings (interest + compounding). APY is typically higher than APR for the same nominal rate.
▶Why does APY increase with more frequent compounding?
More frequent compounding means interest is earned on interest more often, yielding a higher effective rate.
