❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is the difference between APR and APY?
APR is the annual interest rate without compounding. APY includes the effect of compounding. For a 6% APR compounded monthly, the APY is 6.17%. That means $10,000 would earn $617 in a year, not just $600.
▶How do I convert APR to APY?
APY = (1 + APR/n)^n - 1. For 5% APR compounded monthly: APY = (1 + 0.05/12)^12 - 1 = 5.12%. Compounding more frequently increases APY.
▶Why is APY higher than APR?
APY is higher because it includes the effect of compounding — interest earned on interest. For a 5% APR, APY ranges from 5% (annual compounding) to 5.13% (daily compounding).
