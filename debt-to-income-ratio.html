❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is the debt-to-income ratio?
DTI is the percentage of your monthly gross income that goes toward debt payments. It's a key metric lenders use to assess your borrowing capacity.
▶How can I lower my DTI?
You can lower your DTI by increasing your income, paying down existing debt, refinancing to lower monthly payments, or avoiding new debt.
