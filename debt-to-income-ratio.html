❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is a good debt-to-income ratio for a mortgage?
Lenders prefer a DTI of 36% or less. For conventional loans, the maximum is typically 43%. A 36% DTI means your total debt payments are 36% of your gross income.
▶How do I calculate my DTI ratio?
DTI = (Total monthly debt payments ÷ Gross monthly income) × 100. For example, if you earn $5,000/month and pay $1,500 in debts, your DTI is 30%.
▶How can I lower my DTI ratio quickly?
Pay down credit cards and loans, increase your income, or refinance debt to lower monthly payments. A $500 credit card payment eliminated reduces your DTI by 10% on a $5,000 income.
