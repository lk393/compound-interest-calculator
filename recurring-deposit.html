❓ FAQ

▶
▶How much will I have if I deposit $500 per month in RD for 5 years at 6%?
Your total investment would be $30,000. At 6% interest compounded quarterly, your maturity amount would be approximately $34,741, earning you $4,741 in interest.
▶What is the difference between RD and FD?
RD (Recurring Deposit) involves monthly fixed deposits. FD (Fixed Deposit) is a one-time lump sum investment. On $500/month for 5 years at 6%, RD earns $4,741. A $30,000 FD at 6% for 5 years earns $10,384 — FD earns more because the full amount earns interest from day one.
▶Is RD a good investment for beginners?
Yes, RD is excellent for beginners. It encourages regular savings, offers guaranteed returns (typically 5-7%), and has no market risk. A $500/month RD for 3 years at 6% would grow to about $19,682.
