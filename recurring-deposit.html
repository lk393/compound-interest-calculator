❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is a recurring deposit?
A recurring deposit is a savings plan where you deposit a fixed amount every month for a fixed tenure, earning compound interest on your deposits.
▶Is the interest rate fixed?
Yes — recurring deposits typically offer a fixed interest rate for the entire tenure, making them a safe and predictable savings option.
