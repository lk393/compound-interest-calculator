📖 How to read your results

▶

Monthly Interest

The interest that accrues each month on each debt. Higher interest debts cost more to carry.

Which to Pay First

The debt with the highest interest rate should generally be paid off first to minimize total interest paid (avalanche method).

Interest Difference

The difference in annual interest between the two debts. This shows potential savings.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶Should I always pay off the highest interest debt first?
Mathematically, yes — the avalanche method (highest rate first) saves the most money. However, the snowball method (smallest balance first) can be more motivating for some people.
▶What if I have more than two debts?
This calculator compares two debts at a time. Repeat the comparison for each pair to prioritize all your debts.
