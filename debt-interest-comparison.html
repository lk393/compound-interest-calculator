❓ FAQ

▶
▶How much does a 2% interest rate difference cost on a $10,000 loan over 5 years?
A 2% difference (6% vs 8%) costs about $600 extra in interest on a $10,000 5-year loan. At 8%, you pay $2,166 in interest vs $1,600 at 6%.
▶How can I compare two different loan offers?
Compare the APR (includes fees) and total interest cost. A $10,000 loan at 6% with $500 fees costs about $2,100 total. A 6.5% loan with no fees costs about $1,740 — the higher rate but lower fee option is better.
▶What is the interest cost difference between 5% and 7% on a $20,000 mortgage?
On a $20,000 30-year mortgage, 5% costs $18,650 in interest. 7% costs $27,925 in interest. That's a $9,275 difference.
