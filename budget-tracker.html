Income minus total actual expenses. Positive means you're saving money overall.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What's the 50/30/20 rule?
50% needs (housing, food), 30% wants (entertainment), 20% savings. This calculator helps you see if you're on track.
▶How often should I track my budget?
Monthly is ideal. Track regularly to catch overspending early and adjust your habits.
