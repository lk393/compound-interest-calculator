❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is the monthly payment on a $30,000 car loan at 6% for 60 months?
Your monthly payment would be approximately $580. Over 60 months, you would pay a total of $34,799, including $4,799 in interest.
▶How much car can I afford with a $500 monthly payment?
At a 6% interest rate on a 60-month loan, a $500 monthly payment would allow you to borrow about $25,859. With a 10% down payment ($2,800), you could afford a car worth roughly $28,700.
▶Is it better to finance or pay cash for a car?
If the loan rate is below 5% and you can invest your cash at 7%+ returns, financing makes sense. At 6%+ rates, paying cash saves you interest. A $30,000 car at 6% for 5 years costs $4,800 in interest.
▶How does a longer loan term affect my car payment?
A $30,000 loan at 6%: 36-month payment is $912 (total interest $2,847). 60-month payment is $580 (total interest $4,799). 72-month payment is $496 (total interest $5,710). Longer terms lower your monthly payment but cost more in total interest.
