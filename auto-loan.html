❓ FAQ

▶
▶Should I include tax and fees in the loan?
This calculator uses the vehicle price, down payment, and trade-in value. For a more accurate estimate, add sales tax and fees to the vehicle price before entering.
▶What is a good interest rate for an auto loan?
Rates vary based on credit score and market conditions. In 2026, good credit rates range from 5-8% for new cars and 6-10% for used cars.
