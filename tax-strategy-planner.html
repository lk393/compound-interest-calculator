❓ FAQ

▶
▶Is it better to take the standard deduction or itemize?
For 2026, standard deduction is $14,600 (single). If your itemized deductions (mortgage interest, state taxes, charity) exceed $14,600, itemizing saves you more. A $20,000 itemized deduction saves $5,200 more than the standard at 22% tax rate.
▶What deductions can I itemize?
Common itemized deductions include: mortgage interest, state and local taxes (capped at $10,000), charitable contributions, medical expenses (over 7.5% of income), and casualty losses.
▶How much tax can I save by making a $2,000 charitable donation?
At a 22% tax rate, a $2,000 charitable donation saves you $440 in taxes if you itemize. The actual tax savings = donation × your marginal tax rate.
