❓ FAQ

▶
▶Traditional IRA vs Roth IRA: which is better for me?
If you're in the 22% bracket now and expect 15% in retirement, Traditional wins: $10,000 grows to $59,376 after tax vs $56,160 for Roth. If you expect higher taxes (25%), Roth wins.
▶How does a taxable account compare to IRAs?
Taxable accounts have no tax benefits: you pay taxes on contributions and on growth (capital gains). A $10,000 taxable investment at 7% for 20 years yields about $35,000 after taxes, vs $38,700 in a Roth IRA.
▶What is the 2026 IRA contribution limit?
The 2026 IRA contribution limit is $7,000 ($8,000 if age 50+). This applies to both Traditional and Roth IRAs combined.
