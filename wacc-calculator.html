❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is the WACC of a company with 60% equity at 12% cost and 40% debt at 6% after tax?
WACC = (0.60 × 12%) + (0.40 × 6%) = 7.2% + 2.4% = 9.6%. The company needs to earn at least 9.6% on new investments.
▶How does WACC affect company valuation?
WACC is used as the discount rate in DCF valuation. A 1% lower WACC increases a company's value by about 10-15%. For a $100M company, a 1% WACC reduction adds $10-15M in value.
▶What is a good WACC for a stable company?
Stable, low-risk companies have WACC of 6-8%. Growth companies have WACC of 10-15%. A 7% WACC means the company can fund projects that return at least 7%.
