📖 How to read your results

▶

WACC

The minimum return a company must earn on its investments. Used as a discount rate in DCF analysis.

Weight of Equity/Debt

The proportion of total capital represented by each source of financing.

After‑Tax Cost of Debt

The cost of debt reduced by the tax shield. Interest payments are tax‑deductible.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is WACC used for?
WACC is used as a discount rate in discounted cash flow (DCF) analysis and to evaluate investment projects.
▶What is a good WACC?
A lower WACC indicates a company can fund operations cheaply. The "good" value depends on the industry and risk profile.
