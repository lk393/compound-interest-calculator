📖 How to read your results

▶

Total Cost

The sum of the transfer fee and the exchange rate markup (in your home currency). This is the true cost of the transfer.

Markup Cost

The difference between the mid‑market rate and the offered rate, multiplied by the amount. This is the hidden fee.

Effective Rate

The actual exchange rate you get after factoring in the transfer fee. A lower number means worse value.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶How much does it cost to transfer $1,000 internationally with a $10 fee and 2% markup?
Total cost = $10 fee + ($1,000 × 2%) = $10 + $20 = $30. You'll receive $970 worth of foreign currency. The effective exchange rate is 2% worse than the mid-market rate.
▶What is the most cost-effective way to send money abroad?
Compare providers like Wise, Revolut, and OFX. Wise typically charges 0.5-1% total cost, while banks charge 3-5%. On a $1,000 transfer, the difference can be $30-$50.
▶What is the hidden cost in international transfers?
The hidden cost is the exchange rate markup — the difference between the mid-market rate and the rate you're offered. A 2% markup on $5,000 costs you $100 that you never see as a separate fee.
