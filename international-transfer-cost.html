📖 How to read your results

▶

Total Cost

The sum of the transfer fee and the exchange rate markup (in your home currency). This is the true cost of the transfer.

Markup Cost

The difference between the mid‑market rate and the offered rate, multiplied by the amount. This is the hidden fee.

Effective Rate

The actual exchange rate you get after factoring in the transfer fee. A lower number means worse value.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is the mid‑market rate?
The mid‑market rate is the real exchange rate used between banks, with no markup. It's the best possible rate you could get.
▶How can I reduce transfer costs?
Compare providers, choose those with low or zero fees and tight exchange rate spreads. Avoid providers that offer a bad rate to compensate for low fees.
