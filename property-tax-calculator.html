❓ FAQ

▶
▶How much property tax will I pay on a $300,000 home at 1.2% rate?
Annual property tax = $300,000 × 0.012 = $3,600. Monthly = $300. Property tax rates vary by county and state.
▶What is the average property tax rate in the US?
The average effective property tax rate in the US is about 1.07%. Rates range from 0.5% (Hawaii) to over 2.4% (New Jersey). On a $300,000 home, that's $1,500-$7,200 per year.
▶How can I lower my property taxes?
Apply for homestead exemptions, appeal your property assessment if values have declined, or check for senior/veteran discounts. A successful appeal can save $500-$2,000/year.
