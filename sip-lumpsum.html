📖 How to read your results

Final Value

The total value of your investment at the end of the period.

Total Returns

The profit generated by your investment — final value minus total invested.

ROI

The percentage return on your total investment. Higher is better.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶Which is better — SIP or Lump Sum?
It depends on market conditions. In a rising market, lump sum often performs better. In a volatile or falling market, SIP reduces risk through rupee-cost averaging.
▶Does this account for dividends?
No — this calculation assumes price appreciation only. Dividends or distributions would increase returns.
