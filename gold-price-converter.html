❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is a troy ounce?
A troy ounce is 31.1035 grams, used for precious metals. It's different from the standard avoirdupois ounce (28.35g).
▶Can I use this for other metals?
Yes, you can enter any spot price for silver, platinum, or other metals to convert their value.
