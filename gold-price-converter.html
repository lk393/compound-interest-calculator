❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is the value of 1 ounce of gold at $2,000/oz in USD?
1 troy ounce = 31.1035 grams. At $2,000/oz, 1 oz = $2,000. 1 gram = $64.30. A 100g bar would be worth $6,430.
▶How much does 10 grams of gold cost at $2,000/oz?
10 grams = 10 / 31.1035 = 0.3215 ounces. Value = 0.3215 × $2,000 = $643. At $2,000/oz, 10g of gold costs about $643.
▶What is the difference between troy ounce and regular ounce?
A troy ounce (used for precious metals) is 31.1035 grams. A standard avoirdupois ounce is 28.3495 grams. This difference means a troy ounce is 9.7% heavier.
