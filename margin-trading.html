❓ FAQ

▶
▶How much profit do I make on a $10,000 margin trade with 2x leverage and 10% price increase?
With 2x leverage, you control $20,000. A 10% price increase gives $2,000 profit. Your ROI on $10,000 is 20%. Without leverage, it would be just 10%.
▶What happens if the trade goes against me with 2x leverage?
With 2x leverage, a 10% price drop gives a $2,000 loss — your entire $10,000 investment would be wiped out at a 50% drop (margin call). Leverage magnifies both gains and losses.
▶What is a margin call and how do I avoid it?
A margin call happens when your account value falls below the maintenance margin. With 2x leverage, a 50% price drop triggers a margin call. Use stop-loss orders to limit losses.
