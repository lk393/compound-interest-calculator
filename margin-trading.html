📖 How to read your results

▶

Cash Investment Return

The return you would get without borrowing — your equity × asset return.

Margin Return

The return with leverage — your equity returns amplified by the borrowed amount, minus interest costs.

Leverage Ratio

Total investment divided by your equity. Higher leverage means higher risk and reward.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is the risk of margin trading?
Leverage amplifies losses as well as gains. If the asset falls, you can lose more than your initial equity. There's also the risk of a margin call from your broker.
▶What is a typical margin interest rate?
Margin rates vary by broker, typically ranging from 6-12% depending on the amount borrowed and market conditions.
