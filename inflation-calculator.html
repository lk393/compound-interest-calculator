📖 How to read your results

▶

Total Contributions

The total dollar amount you actually deposit over the period (initial + all monthly contributions). This is your baseline without inflation.

Future Nominal Value

The amount you'll need in the future to have the same purchasing power as your contributions today, after accounting for inflation.

Inflation Erosion

The extra dollars you'll need due to inflation. A positive number means rising prices have reduced your purchasing power.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is a typical inflation rate?
Central banks often target 2% annually. Historical averages in the US are around 3-4% over the long term. Use 3% as a conservative estimate.
▶Why does inflation matter for retirement?
If you need $50,000/year to live today, at 3% inflation you'll need over $90,000 in 20 years to maintain the same lifestyle. Inflation is often the biggest risk to long-term savers.
▶How is the future value calculated with monthly contributions?
We compound the initial lump sum and each monthly contribution at the annual inflation rate, assuming contributions are made at the end of each month.
