📖 How to read your results

▶

Cash Value

The estimated accumulated value of an investment‑linked policy at the end of the term, assuming the return rate.

Cost per $1,000

Premium paid per $1,000 of coverage – a useful metric to compare the cost efficiency of policies.

Recommendation

Based on a combined score of coverage, cost, and cash value, the tool suggests which policy may be better for you.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is cash value in insurance?
Cash value is the savings component of permanent life insurance policies. It grows over time and can be borrowed or withdrawn.
▶Which policy should I choose?
Consider your budget, coverage needs, and whether you want an investment component. The comparator helps you weigh these factors.
