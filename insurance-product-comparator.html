❓ FAQ

▶
▶Which is better: term life or whole life insurance?
Term life costs 5-10x less than whole life. A 30-year-old can get $500,000 term for $300/year vs $3,000+ for whole life. Choose term unless you need estate planning.
▶What metrics should I use to compare insurance products?
Compare coverage/premium ratio (leverage), exclusions, and cash value growth. A $500,000 policy at $500/year has a 1,000x leverage ratio — higher is better.
▶How do I decide between two similar insurance policies?
Compare the total premium paid vs coverage amount. For a $500,000 policy, 20-year term at $400/year costs $8,000 total. Whole life at $3,000/year costs $60,000 — a $52,000 difference.
