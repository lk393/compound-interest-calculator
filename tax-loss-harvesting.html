❓ FAQ

▶
▶How much tax can I save by harvesting a $5,000 loss at 22% tax rate?
Tax savings = $5,000 × 22% = $1,100. If you have $5,000 in capital gains, the loss offsets them completely, saving you $1,100 in taxes.
▶What is the limit for deducting capital losses against ordinary income?
The limit is $3,000 per year ($1,500 if married filing separately). Excess losses can be carried forward to future years indefinitely.
▶What is the wash sale rule and how does it affect tax‑loss harvesting?
The wash sale rule disallows a loss deduction if you buy the same or substantially identical security within 30 days before or after the sale. Wait 31 days to repurchase to avoid this. For example, selling a stock at a $2,000 loss and buying it back within 30 days would lose the tax benefit.
▶How much can I save by carrying forward a $10,000 loss at a 22% tax rate?
You can deduct $3,000 per year against ordinary income. In year 1, you save $660 ($3,000 × 22%). The remaining $7,000 carries forward, saving an additional $1,540 over time. Total potential savings: $2,200.
