❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is a safe withdrawal rate?
The safe withdrawal rate is the percentage of your retirement savings you can withdraw each year without running out of money. The classic 4% rule is based on a 30-year retirement period.
▶Should I include inflation?
Yes — the 4% rule assumes you adjust your withdrawals for inflation each year. This calculator uses today's dollars as a baseline estimate.
