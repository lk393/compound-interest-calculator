❓ FAQ

▶
▶How does a $1,800 rent compare to a mortgage on a $300,000 home at 6.5%?
Mortgage payment = $1,896. With taxes ($250) + insurance ($100) + maintenance ($250) = $2,496. Rent is $1,800 — $696/month cheaper. But buying builds equity.
▶What is the break-even point between renting and buying?
The break-even is usually 5-7 years. If you stay longer, buying is better. For a $300,000 home with $1,800 rent, break-even is about 6 years.
▶What costs make a mortgage payment higher than rent?
Mortgage payments include principal, interest, property taxes, homeowners insurance, and maintenance. These add 30-50% to the base mortgage payment.
