❓ FAQ

▶
▶How much do I need to save each month to reach $50,000 in 5 years?
At a 4% annual return, you'd need to save about $755 per month. Without any return, you'd need $833 per month.
▶How long will it take to save $10,000 if I save $500 a month?
Without interest, it would take 20 months. With a 4% annual return, it would take about 19 months.
▶What is the best way to save for a house down payment?
Set a specific goal (e.g., $60,000 for a 20% down payment on a $300,000 home). Saving $1,000 monthly at 4% return would reach this goal in about 54 months.
