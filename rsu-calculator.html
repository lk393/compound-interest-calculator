❓ FAQ

▶
▶How much tax will I pay on 1,000 RSUs vesting at $50/share with a 32% tax rate?
Value at vesting = 1,000 × $50 = $50,000. Tax = $50,000 × 32% = $16,000. After-tax value = $34,000.
▶How are RSUs taxed?
RSUs are taxed as ordinary income at vesting. The taxable amount is the fair market value on the vesting date. If 100 RSUs vest at $50, you owe tax on $5,000 of income.
▶Should I sell RSUs immediately upon vesting?
Many advisors recommend selling immediately to diversify. If you keep them and the stock rises from $50 to $60, you gain $10/share but also risk losses. Tax is due at vesting regardless of whether you sell.
