❓ FAQ

▶
▶What are RSUs?
Restricted Stock Units are company shares granted to employees that vest over time. You receive actual shares at vesting and pay ordinary income tax on them.
▶How are RSUs taxed?
RSUs are taxed as ordinary income at vesting. The taxable amount is the fair market value of the shares on the vesting date.
