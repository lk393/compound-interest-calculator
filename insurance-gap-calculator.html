❓ FAQ

▶
▶How is the insurance gap calculated?
Insurance Gap = Ideal Coverage - Current Coverage. Ideal coverage is typically 5-10x your annual income plus total debts.
▶What is the average insurance gap for a family with a mortgage?
For a family earning $80,000 with a $300,000 mortgage, ideal coverage is about $700,000-$1,100,000. With $200,000 existing coverage, the gap is $500,000-$900,000.
▶What should I do if my insurance gap is too large?
Start with term life insurance to cover the largest gap — it's affordable. A healthy 30-year-old can get $500,000 of 20-year term coverage for $300-$500/year.
