📖 How to read your results

▶

Net Worth

Your total net worth = Total Assets − Total Liabilities. This is the single best measure of your overall financial health.

Assets

Everything you own that has value — cash, investments, property, and other assets.

Liabilities

Everything you owe — mortgages, credit cards, student loans, and other debts.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What should I include as assets?
Include cash, bank accounts, investment accounts, retirement accounts, real estate, vehicles, and any other valuable possessions you could sell.
▶What should I include as liabilities?
Include mortgages, car loans, student loans, credit card balances, personal loans, and any other outstanding debts.
