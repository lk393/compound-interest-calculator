❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is a good net worth for a 30-year-old earning $60,000?
A common rule is to have your age × annual salary / 10. For a 30-year-old earning $60,000, a good target net worth is about $180,000 (30 × $60,000 ÷ 10).
▶What should I include in my net worth calculation?
Assets include: cash, investments, retirement accounts, real estate value, and personal property. Liabilities include: mortgage, car loans, student loans, and credit card debt.
▶What is the average net worth in the US?
The average net worth in the US is about $748,800 (2024 data). The median net worth is about $166,900. High earners and older Americans typically have significantly higher net worth.
