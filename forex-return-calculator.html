The contribution of exchange rate change to the total return. Positive means the foreign currency strengthened.

Asset Effect

The return from the foreign asset itself, independent of the exchange rate.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶How does exchange rate affect returns?
A strengthening foreign currency increases your return when converted back to home currency, and vice versa.
▶What is the currency effect?
It's the portion of your return due solely to exchange rate movement. It can be positive or negative.
