📖 How to read your results

▶

Fee Drag

The total dollar amount you lose to high fees compared to the low-fee option. This is money that could have grown your portfolio.

Savings

The amount you save by choosing the lower-fee investment option. Even small fee differences compound significantly over time.

Final Value

The ending portfolio value after accounting for fees. Lower fees mean more money stays in your account.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is a reasonable management fee?
For index funds and ETFs, fees are often 0.03-0.20%. Actively managed funds may charge 0.5-1.5%. Lower fees generally lead to higher net returns over time.
▶Why do fees matter so much?
Fees compound just like investment returns. A 1% fee can reduce your final portfolio value by 20-30% over 30 years because you lose both the fees and the growth they could have earned.
