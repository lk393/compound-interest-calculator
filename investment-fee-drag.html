❓ FAQ

▶
▶How much does a 1% management fee cost me on $100,000 over 30 years?
A 1% fee on $100,000 at 7% gross return costs about $280,000 over 30 years. With the fee, your final value is $574,000 vs $762,000 without — you lose 37% of your potential returns.
▶What is the difference between a 0.1% fee and a 1% fee over 20 years?
On $100,000 at 7% gross return, 0.1% fee gives $376,000 after 20 years, while 1% fee gives $331,000. The 0.9% difference costs you $45,000.
▶What is a reasonable expense ratio for index funds?
Good index funds have expense ratios of 0.03-0.20%. Vanguard's S&P 500 ETF (VOO) has 0.03% — just $3 per $10,000 invested. Active funds typically charge 0.5-1.5%.
