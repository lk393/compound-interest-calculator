❓ FAQ

▶
▶Which debt strategy pays off debt the fastest?
The avalanche method (highest interest first) is mathematically the fastest and saves the most money. On $15,000 in debt, avalanche pays off 3-6 months faster than snowball.
▶How much more interest does snowball cost vs avalanche on $20,000?
On $20,000 debt across 4 accounts, snowball typically costs $800-$1,500 more in interest than avalanche. The difference depends on how varied your interest rates are.
▶What is the best debt payoff strategy for beginners?
Snowball is best for beginners because it provides quick motivation wins. Paying off a $1,000 debt quickly feels rewarding and keeps you going.
