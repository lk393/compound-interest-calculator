📖 How to read your results

▶

Snowball

Pays off the smallest balance first. Provides quick wins and motivation, but may cost more in interest.

Avalanche

Pays off the highest interest rate first. Saves the most money on interest, but may take longer to see progress.

Winner

The strategy that saves you the most money. Avalanche usually wins mathematically, but Snowball may be better for motivation.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶Which strategy is better?
Avalanche saves more money mathematically. Snowball is better for motivation and momentum. Choose the one you'll actually stick with.
▶Can I use both strategies?
Yes — some people start with Snowball for motivation on small debts, then switch to Avalanche for larger debts. The best strategy is the one you follow through.
