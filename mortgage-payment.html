❓ FAQ

▶
▶ What is the formula used for the monthly payment?
It uses the standard amortization formula: M = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the total number of payments.
▶ What does the amortization schedule show?
It shows how your balance decreases over time. In the early years, most of your payment goes toward interest; later, more goes toward principal.
▶ Does this include property taxes or insurance?
No — this calculator covers only the principal and interest portion of your mortgage. Property taxes, homeowners insurance, and PMI are not included.
▶ Can I use this for an adjustable-rate mortgage (ARM)?
This calculator assumes a fixed interest rate. For ARMs, the rate changes over time, so this would only be an estimate for the initial fixed period.
