❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is the formula used for the monthly payment?
It uses the standard amortization formula: M = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n − 1).
▶Does this include property taxes or insurance?
No — this calculator covers only the principal and interest portion of your mortgage.
