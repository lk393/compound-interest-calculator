❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is the ROI on a $50,000 investment that returns $65,000?
ROI = ($65,000 - $50,000) / $50,000 × 100 = 30%. Your profit is $15,000 on a $50,000 investment.
▶What is a good ROI percentage?
For the S&P 500, average ROI is 7-10% per year. For real estate, 8-12% is considered good. For marketing campaigns, 5× ROI (500%) is a common target.
▶How do I calculate annualized ROI?
Annualized ROI = (1 + Total ROI)^(1/Years) - 1. A 30% ROI over 3 years = (1.30)^(1/3) - 1 = 9.1% per year.
