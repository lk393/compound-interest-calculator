📖 How to read your results

▶

Total Return (ROI)

The total percentage gain on your investment. Calculated as (Final Value − Initial Investment) / Initial Investment × 100%.

Annualized Return (CAGR)

The equivalent yearly return that would produce the same total growth over the investment period. Useful for comparing investments of different lengths.

Total Profit

The dollar amount you gained — simply the difference between the final value and your initial investment.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶ What is the difference between ROI and CAGR?
ROI is the total return over the entire holding period, while CAGR is the annualized return that smooths out volatility to a steady yearly growth rate. CAGR is better for comparing investments of different durations.
▶ Does this include dividends or distributions?
No — this calculator assumes you only track the change in capital value. For dividend-paying investments, you would need to include reinvested dividends as part of the final value.
▶ Can I use this for real estate investments?
Yes — you can enter the purchase price as the initial investment and the current market value as the final value. Be sure to consider transaction costs separately.
▶ What if the investment lost money?
The calculator handles negative returns automatically — ROI and CAGR will be negative, and the profit will be displayed in red to indicate a loss.
