❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is the difference between ROI and CAGR?
ROI is the total return over the entire holding period, while CAGR is the annualized return.
▶Does this include dividends?
No — this assumes you only track the change in capital value. For dividend-paying investments, include reinvested dividends in the final value.
