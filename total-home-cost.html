❓ FAQ

▶
▶What is the total cost of owning a $300,000 home over 30 years at 6.5%?
Total cost = purchase price ($300,000) + interest ($382,633) + taxes ($90,000) + insurance ($36,000) + maintenance ($90,000) = $898,633. The true cost is almost 3× the purchase price.
▶How much does maintenance cost on a home each year?
A common rule is 1% of the home's value annually. For a $300,000 home, that's $3,000/year or $250/month. Over 30 years, that's $90,000 in maintenance costs.
▶What should I budget for homeownership costs beyond the mortgage?
Budget an additional 1-3% of your home's value annually. On a $300,000 home, that's $3,000-$9,000/year for taxes, insurance, and maintenance combined.
