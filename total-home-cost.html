📖 How to read your results

▶

Mortgage Interest

The total interest paid over the life of the loan — often the largest cost of homeownership.

Operating Costs

Property taxes, insurance, and maintenance are ongoing costs that add up significantly over time.

Total Cost

The sum of all costs: purchase price, interest, taxes, insurance, and maintenance. This is the true cost of owning the home.

❓ FAQ

▶
▶What costs are included?
This calculator includes mortgage interest, property taxes, insurance, and maintenance/repairs. It does not include utility costs or HOA fees.
▶What is the 1% rule for maintenance?
A common rule of thumb is to budget 1% of the home's value annually for maintenance and repairs. This calculator uses a fixed amount you can adjust.
